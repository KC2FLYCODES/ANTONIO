Before you start

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. Past work runs from a $4,000 attic build-out to whole-house renovations. Pick "Something else" and describe it.

Do I need an exact budget?

No. A rough range helps us come with the right ideas. "Not sure yet" is a fine answer.

Do I have to send photos?

No, but a couple of phone shots of the area help us understand the job before we visit.

Do you work with investors and realtors?

Yes. The Starmount Forest remodel came through a local realtor and investor. Tell us that's you in the questions.
